# Published equipment measurements

The numerical Surrey microphone response adaptations in `published.json` derive
from the Multi-Angle, Multi-Distance Microphone Impulse Response Dataset,
Juan Carlos Franco Hernández, Tim Brookes and Enzo De Sena, version 1.0.0,
DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4633508, licensed CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes by SoundCurrent: on-axis 1.25 m magnitude ratio against the same-dataset
NTi M2211 reference, restricted coverage, smoothing, normalization and bounded
inverse-filter fitting. This is not certified individual-unit calibration.
Attribution, exact source members and hashes are retained in every profile.

Other new numerical responses are independently read from published measurement
graphs; the primary sources, configurations and readout uncertainty are identified
in each profile and `published-collection-report.json`. No original graph artwork,
PDFs, article prose, or unlicensed third-party aggregate catalog is distributed.
The source publishers retain their rights in those original works.

See `docs/equipment-profiles.md` for methods, exclusions and original source links.
SoundCurrent software and its own correction-generation code are GPL-3.0-only.
