VB-CABLE is a separate proprietary driver by VB-Audio Software (Vincent Burel).
Origin and donation/license information: https://vb-cable.com and https://vb-audio.com/Services/licensing.htm
VB-CABLE is donationware; donations are welcome. It is not part of SoundCurrent's GPL license.
The original primary cable package is included unchanged under VB-Audio's published distribution rules. Professional deployments must observe VB-Audio's licensing terms. A+B and C+D are not bundled.
The official setup needs administrator approval. Reboot after installation/removal.
SoundCurrent's last-app uninstaller offers removal; other software/users may need the shared cable. Silent app removal keeps it. Open Audio driver setup to retry driver setup, and VB-CABLE settings for latency/sample rate.
